Cloakwing is a nocturnal, urban-dwelling Pokémon known for its shadowy tactics and scrappy brawling style. Noctisaur

◐Dark ⬟Fighting

About Noctisaur

Noctisaur is a powerful, imposing martial artist of the dark. Upon evolution, it sheds its stealthy hood in favour of a heavy, crimson-lined mantle that acts both as armor and as a tool to throw opponents off balance during close-quarters combat. It commands respect in the wild, using its immense upper-body strength and wing-assisted mobility to protect its territory. Highly honorable, it follows a strict code of combat and will rarely attack an opponent that is unprepared or unable to fight back.

Pokédex entries

Entry 1. Having fully matured, Noctisaur replaces its early hood with a weighted cloak, using the heavy fabric to obscure its movements and disrupt enemy parries. Its dense, wrapped fists impact with enough force to shatter solid concrete in a single blow.

Entry 2. Noctisaur strictly adheres to an unwritten code of combat, refusing to strike an unarmed or weakened foe. Using its broad wingspan to glide silently through the night air, it descends on targets with swift, relentless martial precision.

The fully evolved form of Noxling, Jesterror is a sinister, theatrical mastermind that thrives entirely on disorder. Possessing sharp intelligence and a wicked flair for the dramatic, it treats entire cities as its personal stage, orchestrating complex schemes just to watch the chaos unfold.

Pokédex entries

Scarlet. It hurls corrosive, spiked poison-bombs attached to its tail vines to destabilise structure and order. It orchestrates elaborate, high-stakes schemes across regions for the sole purpose of baiting its hated arch-rival, Noctisaur.

Violet. Its intellect matches its intense malice. Using hypnotic pulses generated by its collar and wings, it can incite wild panic in crowds. Though it plans meticulously to bring cities to their knees, Noctisaur routinely foils its grand finales—an endless humiliation that only deepens its obsession.

Meowmet

About Meowmet

Meowmet is a timid feline Pokémon that originally lacked any natural defensive armor. Driven by a desire to look heroic, it found a discarded metal pot, punched eyeholes through the side, and strapped it onto its head alongside a torn red scarf. Violet / Blue. The pot on its head acts as both a shield and a source of bravery. If stripped of its makeshift helmet, its confidence shatters, leaving it entirely unable to fight.
